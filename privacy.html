Privacy Policy

Cited Cents Uploader · last updated 25 October 2026 · Home

Cited Cents Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the Cited Cents YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services and is used only with that owner's own Google account. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the tool accesses

Google permission (scope)Why
Upload videos to YouTube (youtube.upload)Upload the channel's own videos and set their metadata and schedule.
View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly)Check upload and publishing status, and read public comments on the channel's own videos.
View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly)Read the channel's own analytics: views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources, countries.

The tool does not request access to revenue data, payment information, or any account other than the owner's channel.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to decide what to make next. It is not sold, rented, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.
  • Everything is stored on the owner's own computer, not on any server operated by the tool. Login tokens are kept in a protected local folder and are never published.
  • Channel statistics are kept so trends can be compared over time. The tool checks at least every 30 days that access is still authorised and that videos still exist.
  • Public comment text is deleted after 30 days. Commenter names and channel IDs are not stored.

Your choices

  • You can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, stored Google user data is deleted within 30 days.
  • To ask a question or request deletion of data, email citedcents@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
