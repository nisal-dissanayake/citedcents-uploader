Cited Cents Uploader

The science behind money and the mind, with every claim cited.

Cited Cents Uploader is a small desktop tool that belongs to one YouTube channel, Cited Cents. It is not a public app and has no other users.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the Cited Cents channel and sets their title, description and scheduled publish time.
  • Reads the channel's own, read-only analytics (views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources) so the channel can learn what viewers watch.
  • Reads public comments on the channel's videos to find questions that could become future videos.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, upload or change anything on any other channel or account.
  • It does not like, subscribe, comment or message anyone, and it does not sell or share data.
